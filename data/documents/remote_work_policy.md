# Acme Cloud — Remote Work Policy

Version 1.3. Owner: People Operations (people@acmecloud.example). This is a demo document for the RAG chatbot:
pick it in the chat box to ask questions about remote and hybrid work.

## Who can work remotely
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probation can work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Fully remote roles are marked as such in the job offer and do not need extra approval.
Employees on probation work from the office at least 4 days per week so they can learn the team's ways of working.

Support engineers on the on-call rota must be reachable within 15 minutes during their shift, wherever they work.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 in the employee's local time zone. Meetings should be scheduled inside
core hours. Outside core hours, employees may organise their own time as long as they work their contracted hours.

Status in the chat tool should show "Remote" on remote days and "Away" when stepping out for more than 30 minutes.

## Home office stipend
Every employee eligible for remote work receives a one-time home office stipend of 400 USD to buy a desk, a chair or
a monitor. The stipend is claimed through the expense tool within 6 months of becoming eligible. Receipts are required.

Remote employees also receive an internet allowance of 30 USD per month, paid with salary. Employees who work fully
remotely receive 50 USD per month instead.

## Equipment and security
Company laptops must be used for all work. Personal devices may only be used for email and chat through the approved
mobile apps. Always connect through the company VPN when using public Wi-Fi, for example in cafes, hotels or airports.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT support (it@acmecloud.example) within 24 hours.
Never leave a company laptop unattended in a car or a public place.

## Working from another country
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Requests must be submitted to
People Operations at least 14 days before travel, because tax and visa rules differ between countries.
Working from abroad does not change the employee's salary, public holidays or leave allowance.

## Requesting remote days
Remote days are agreed with the manager in the team calendar at least one day ahead. A manager can ask an employee to
come to the office for team events, onboarding new colleagues or customer visits, with at least 3 days' notice.

## Questions
Contact People Operations at people@acmecloud.example or ask in the #remote-work channel.
